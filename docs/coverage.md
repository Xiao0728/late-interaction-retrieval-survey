# Coverage and provenance

The complete bibliography reconciles **120 distinct literature/resource records** with the authors’ August 2026 manuscript snapshot (59 PDF pages including front matter). The public survey is [SSRN 7412000](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7412000). The live SSRN PDF was not independently compared with this snapshot.

README reading lists use one table per chapter (§§3–11), with background and boundary cases collected at the end. README selection is based on substantive contributions, not every citation occurrence. Backbone reuse and baseline comparisons do not justify extra rows. Papers may recur across chapters only for distinct contributions; each placement has a rationale in `data/reading-list-selection.json`. General references can remain bibliography-only. Subsection mappings and the distinction between explicit citations and named discussions are retained in the audit data. See the [citation reconciliation report](citation-audit.md) for evidence, duplicate handling, and validation.

[survey.bib](../survey.bib) and [CITATION.cff](../CITATION.cff) cite the survey itself. [references.bib](../references.bib) exports the surveyed literature, including contextual and software references. Publication years follow the surveyed reference version; a linked preprint may have a different initial posting year or later publication. Generic BibTeX entries preserve title, authors, year, URL, and available venue information without inferring missing publication fields.

Paper URLs come from the source bibliography or primary publication records. Completeness against this snapshot does not imply independent verification of every empirical claim or continuous external-link availability. The survey DOI remains omitted until verified; its SSRN abstract URL is used instead.

Future additions must distinguish manuscript-covered literature from post-survey additions. A revised manuscript requires fresh body-to-bibliography reconciliation, not merely regeneration of the existing manifest.
