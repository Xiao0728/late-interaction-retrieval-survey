## Initial release

The initial reading list contains 76 distinct research entries. Section mapping and short mechanism descriptions were prepared from the authors' available August 2026 manuscript snapshot (59 PDF pages including front matter). The public paper entry is [SSRN 7412000](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7412000).

The live SSRN PDF was not independently compared with that snapshot during repository initialization. This collection therefore does not claim an exact, exhaustive synchronization with every reference in the posted PDF.

Primary paper URLs were taken from the manuscript bibliography or resolved through primary publication records. Software links were taken from the cited projects and their documentation. This is bibliographic curation, not a fresh independent verification of every paper's empirical claims or every external URL's availability.

Publication labels identify the reference version used by the survey; an arXiv label does not assert that no later publication exists. The survey's own DOI is deliberately omitted until verified on its live citation record. Its stable SSRN abstract URL is used instead.

## Organization

Entries have one primary section and may affect additional components. Sections 2–3 supply definitions and the prototype; Sections 4–10 organize interventions; Section 11 includes research directions and contextual benchmarks. Agentic retrieval and neighboring multi-vector methods are labeled as context or boundary cases.

The initial release is a selected collection, not a reproduction of the full manuscript or bibliography. Software resources and benchmark links are maintained separately from the count of reading-list entries.

## Future additions

Record whether an addition is already covered by the survey or extends beyond the survey snapshot. When moving to a revised survey or a journal version, reconcile section numbering, publication metadata, duplicates, and the preferred citation together.
